    How to Play

    Blackjack where every win banks a bonus multiplier for your next hand. Keep winning and the multipliers climb with your streak, all the way up to 100x on a Blackjack.
    1
    Start a heaterYour stake splits 50/50. Half rides on the hand, half is the Heater Fee.
    2
    Win to bank a multiplierEach round rolls six multipliers, one per winning total. The one for your total is banked.
    3
    Win again to cash inThe banked bonus ignites onto your bet as the next hand starts, and a win is paid at it. For example 5x on $10 pays $50 plus your bet back.
    Ride the heater

    Heat builds with each won round, up to 5. Your bet is locked while a heater runs. It ends on a loss, push, dealer blackjack or when you reset.

    Heater Fee

    Half of your stake is the Heater Fee. It is never returned, whether you win, push or lose. Double and split add only the extra bet, never an extra fee. The fee pays for the multipliers.

    Bonus Multipliers

    Before every hand the game rolls six multipliers for your heat: 4–17, 18, 19, 20, 21 and Blackjack. They never decrease going up the list, and Blackjack is always at least 1.5x the 21 row. Higher heat means higher multipliers, up to 100x. A banked multiplier replaces the normal 1:1 payout on your next winning hand, and is used once. On a blackjack you get the multiplier or 3:2, whichever is higher. A loss, push or dealer blackjack forfeits it.

    Splits, Doubles and Insurance

    Double on any first two cards, including after a split; the multiplier pays on your original bet and the extra pays 1:1. Split once only; split Aces get one card each, and 21 after a split counts as 21, not blackjack. A split round counts as one win if either hand wins, and a multiplier pays on one winning hand only. Insurance is offered when the dealer shows an Ace, costs half your bet and pays 2:1. It never counts as a win and the streak still resets if the dealer has blackjack.

    Table Rules

    Unlimited decks. Dealer stands on all 17s. A dealer blackjack ends the round immediately. Blackjack pays 3:2, or the active multiplier if that is higher. One hand per round, no side bets.

    Return to Player

    Target RTP is 99%. In this prototype the multiplier values are placeholders and have not been tuned.
