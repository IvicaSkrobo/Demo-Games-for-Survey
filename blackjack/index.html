Classic Blackjack

Blackjack pays 3 to 2

BETPLAY CHIPS
Test special cards

Deal a hand, then choose a card to test.

Table rules

Beat the dealer without going over 21. Aces count as 1 or 11. The dealer stands on all 17s. Double your bet on your first two cards to draw exactly one more card. Ties return your bet. Split matching ranks once with an equal bet. Split aces get one card each. In Daredevil, tap Hit for a chance to draw Blind Bet, Minus One Joker, Expose, Devil’s Due, or Red or Black. Guess the next card’s color. A correct guess lets you keep or discard it. A wrong guess forces you to keep it. Then you stand. Blind Bet pays 5 to 1 on a win if you take the hidden card. Expose reveals the dealer’s hidden card. Five copies of Devil’s Due can force two draws in a row. At most one special card activates per round.
