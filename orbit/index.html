    How to play

    Type a stake in euros or tap the + chips to add to it. Tap the €1 chip to reset the stake to €1. Changing the stake applies to your next bet.

    Every tap on a fruit places one bet at the displayed stake. Tap the same fruit repeatedly to stack bets, or tap Random bet to pick a random fruit each time.

    Watch the step bar. During Bet, your taps enter the current round. Charge boosts the payout if that fruit lands. Result shows what paid after the wheel stops. Taps during Charge, Harvest and Result go into the next round.

    A gold Harvest landing sends every bet to one bonus reveal. Each fruit has its own reward at that reveal. Cherries always pay something in Harvest. Watermelon can pay more or bust. All payouts settle automatically and include the original stake where applicable.

    Amounts use a demo EUR balance. The wheel shows totals from live bets, with your locked bets highlighted in green. Other riders are simulated.
