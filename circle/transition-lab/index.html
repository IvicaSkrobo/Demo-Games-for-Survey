Real GameCanvasV1, slot-1 (Classic) settings from src/config/variants.ts — actual arc ladder, glow, spin, ball physics. Each button both picks and fires a style. Vanja's eat is the only one this page does not draw — it is the renderer's own sweep (zoneSweep.ts), played on a single canvas that never remounts, so it is literally what /game does on a mode switch: the outgoing ladder is eaten away 0°→360° (400ms), the ring sits completely bare (110ms), then the incoming one is built on from the same 0° (600ms), with each skull slamming in as the edge reaches its own arc. Niki's reform: old ring collapses to a gray dot at center while the new one grows back out of the same point, already in its own colors. Arc equalize: every arc contracts to its layout's own 2x-arc width, spreads to a bigger radius and turns white, holds there, then the new layout's arcs slide out of them into their real widths/colors/radius — one splits into several where the new layout has more, several converge into one where it has fewer, never crossing each other. No crossfade at either end: the overlay's first and last frame are drawn with the real renderer's own band radius, arc width, end gap and ring angle, so they are the same picture the canvas hands over. Standalone route, nothing wired into StudyRoot.
