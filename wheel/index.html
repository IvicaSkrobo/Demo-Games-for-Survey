Rules How to play

A colour bet pays when a field of that colour is hit at the arrow. Your token and amount appear on its colour button. A number bet covers one exact field. Each field prints its base payout.

Every shot your bet survives grows its multiplier by 10%. A skull sweeps every riding bet. A jackpot shot multiplies winning payouts by five.

Bets start riding immediately. If a shot is already revealing, new bets join after it lands. Pump adds fresh stake at base pay; the existing stake keeps its growth.

Heat mode shows the next fields expected to fire. During fast surges, the arrow holds fire.

Play-money visual prototype. Other players are simulated. Entry timing affects outcomes; 95% RTP is not established for this mode.
