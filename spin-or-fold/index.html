    How to play
    1. A new round starts every 7 seconds. Place bets while the green bar runs.
    2. Choose a chip and tap a bet. Tap again to add more. Rebet repeats your last bet.
    3. When betting closes, three balls drop onto the wheel for everyone at the table.
    4. Each ball that lands on your pick counts. More balls, bigger win. Balls can land in the same pocket.
    5. The Joker is wild. It counts for every suit, Red, Black and High, but not for an exact card or a rank.

    Wins are multipliers of your bet, stake included. Red, Black and High need at least 2 balls. The other players in this prototype are simulated.
